# FinEdge Bank — Imagery Asset Register (Module 33)

This register logs every photographic asset used across FinEdge Bank, documenting the master resolution, source, licensing, placement, responsive variants, and verified caption.

## Quality & Sourcing Standards (Module 33 Rev 2)
1. **Real Photography Only**: Licensed candid documentary photography in natural light and authentic Indian context. No AI-generated imagery, no illustrations, no watermarked comps.
2. **4K–8K Masters**: Long edge $\ge 3840$px for full-width/slider-main images; $\ge 2560$px for detail-page and support photographs.
3. **Responsive WebP Variants**: Derived directly from the single master in one encoding pass at photographic quality (WebP quality $\ge 80$).
4. **Honest Captions**: Plain scene descriptions or verified site facts only (`1800 267 4100`). Zero marketing quotes, zero invented testimonials, zero banned regulatory buzzwords.
5. **Palette Family Grading**: Midnight duotone on dark surfaces; natural gently-warmed grading on light surfaces.
